My Little Proposal

Hi love,

Since our monthsary is coming up on October 14, I have a small proposal for us.

I don't want us to spend money or buy expensive gifts. Honestly, the only thing I really want is something meaningful.

I was hoping we could exchange letters with each other. They don't have to be long, perfect, or fancy. Just something honest and from the heart.

We can write about our favorite memories, the things we appreciate about each other, what we've learned together, and our hopes for the future.

I think letters are special because they stay with us. Years from now, we can read them again and remember exactly how we felt at this moment.

So for our monthsary, my proposal is simple: let's exchange letters and give each other something we'll always be able to keep.

No matter what happens, I'm already grateful that I get to celebrate another monthsary with you.

Love,
Bianca ❤️
